EUROPA · RIDGE BASE

Test base b19-story-crisp: same as b18-wall-story (crew portraits on the cabin back wall, Crew Wall teleport pad, 9-page Story screen, NASA/ESA science credit, quiet lander, local radio, starship orbit fix, smoked window tint), with one change — the Story board is now a sharp 2048×1152 display: higher resolution, mipmapped anisotropic filtering, a solid matte background, no thin hairlines, and larger high-contrast type so the story text stays crisp and steady in the headset. The Science screen and everything else are unchanged.
Teleport: point a controller at a glowing ring or its label and pull the trigger (VR), or keys 1–9, 0 and C, the buttons at the bottom, or click a ring (desktop). Rings: 1 Cabin · 2 Front railing · 3 Rear railing · 4 Left edge · 5 Right edge · 6 Glass floor · 7 Living quarters · 8 Table · 9 Roof deck · 0 Lander · C Crew Wall.
Music: click or trigger the music screen on the cabin wall or the quarters' back wall (or press B/Y in VR) to open the music panel: built-in tracks, local files, previous/next, volume − / +, mute, and rig sound on/off. Desktop also has the slider and an Add local audio button bottom-right. Rig sound: M key or the button bottom-right. N next track, P previous.
Desktop: drag to look. Click anywhere outside the button to look around without VR. Drag and drop songs onto this page (computer only).

Serve over http://localhost (or any https) — WebXR needs a secure context.
Jupiter: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS JunoCam. Stars: Hipparcos catalog. Music: "Two Moons Rising (atmospheric)" and "Rings of Gold (atmospheric)" by Mars.
