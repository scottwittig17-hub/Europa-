Ridge Base

EUROPA · RIDGE BASE

Test base b9 (b4 + Mars's "Two Moons Rising" music with volume control) — walk the base by teleporting between glowing rings: the cabin, the front and rear railings, both deck edges, the glass floor and the living quarters upstairs, while the E-7 mining rig crawls slowly along the trench below.
In VR: point a controller at a ring or its label and pull the trigger. Desktop: keys 1–7, the buttons at the bottom, or click a ring.
Music: the slider bottom-right on desktop; in VR look down for the music panel (B or Y hides it).
Desktop: drag to look. Click anywhere outside the button to look around without VR.

Serve over http://localhost (or any https) — WebXR needs a secure context.
Jupiter: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS JunoCam. Stars: Hipparcos catalog. Music: "Two Moons Rising (atmospheric)" by Mars.
