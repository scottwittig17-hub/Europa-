BLACK SAND BEACH

Night. Two moons over the water.
Desktop: drag to look, click the soft glow on the blanket to lie down (click again, the Stand up button, or Esc to get up).
VR: point at the glow and pull the trigger; trigger again or B/Y to stand. Stick left/right snap-turns 30°.
