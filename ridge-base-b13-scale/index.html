Ridge Base

EUROPA · RIDGE BASE

Test base b13-scale: b12 with the gold lander 1.35x bigger and the E-7 rig 1.1x bigger (the most it can grow and still pass under the deck). Everything else as b12 — everything from b5–b11 in one: the E-7 rig crawls the trench with rolling treads and its grinding sound, stays in sight far down the trench both ways, a gold science lander sits on the ice beside the base with its bay open, a ship orbits slowly overhead, and music plays (Two Moons Rising or Rings of Gold).
Teleport: point a controller at a glowing ring or its label and pull the trigger (VR), or keys 1–9 and 0, the buttons at the bottom, or click a ring (desktop). Rings: 1 Cabin · 2 Front railing · 3 Rear railing · 4 Left edge · 5 Right edge · 6 Glass floor · 7 Living quarters · 8 Table · 9 Roof deck · 0 Lander.
Music: click or trigger the music screen on the cabin wall or the quarters' back wall (or press B/Y in VR) to open the music panel: pick the track, volume − / +, mute, and rig sound on/off. Desktop also has the slider bottom-right. Rig sound: M key or the button bottom-right.
Desktop: drag to look. Click anywhere outside the button to look around without VR.

Serve over http://localhost (or any https) — WebXR needs a secure context.
Jupiter: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS JunoCam. Stars: Hipparcos catalog. Music: "Two Moons Rising (atmospheric)" and "Rings of Gold (atmospheric)" by Mars.
